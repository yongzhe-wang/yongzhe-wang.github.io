About

I'm the founder and CEO of General Sequences, which is exploring inference infrastructure for physical AI.

Before General Sequences, I was a Member of Technical Staff at Noematrix, where I worked on pre-training world action models (see the Noe-0 research preview). Earlier, I was a founding engineer at FocusAlpha AI, building AI agents for investment research.

On the research side, I work on vision language action models and world models for dexterous manipulation at Penn's GMLR Lab. Before that, I worked on optimal transport at UNC Chapel Hill, which led to a paper on measure transfer via stochastic slicing and matching.

I studied at the University of Pennsylvania, for a BA in Mathematics and Computer Science and an MSE in Robotics. I like building things quickly: Vertices won first place at the Anthropic × Penn AI Hackathon, and Trick-or-Treat Sorter took third at the Viam × MongoDB Robot Hackathon.

Outside of work, I'm a dancer, and I like drawing elephants and camels.
